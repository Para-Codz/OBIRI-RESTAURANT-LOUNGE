About OBIRI

Where Fine Taste Meets
Vibrant Highlife Rhythm

OBIRI Restaurant & Lounge was established with a singular vision: to create an oasis of culinary excellence and live acoustic music in the heart of Port Harcourt. Inspired by the rich traditions of Nigerian hospitality, we merge local culinary heritage with high-end premium dining.

"Our mission is to deliver unforgettable dining experiences through rich, authentic Nigerian flavors and vibrant, soul-stirring entertainment."

Whether you are stopping by for a swift lunch, holding a birthday blowout, or lounging over fresh calabashes of palm wine on Live Band Fridays, we ensure every moment is crafted to perfection.
